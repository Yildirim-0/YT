Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

YT is a personal tool that runs on the channel owner's own computer. It uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data is accessed

With the permission you grant through Google sign-in (scope youtube.force-ssl), YT reads:

  • your channel's name and upload list,
  • the title, description, tags, privacy status and scheduled time of your recent videos.

What it does with the data

  • Updates the description and scheduled publish time of your own private videos.
  • Posts a comment on your own videos after they are published.

Data is used only for these actions. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Where data is stored

The sign-in token and a small status file (video IDs, titles, scheduled times) are stored only on your own computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's YouTube API.

Removing access

You can revoke YT's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the program folder removes all locally stored data.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Yildirim-0/YT.
